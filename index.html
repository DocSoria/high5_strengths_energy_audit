Where do your strengths show up?

Think about a typical recent workweek. Each prompt offers examples—not a complete definition—of how a strength might show up. A single activity can draw on several strengths, so your estimated percentages do not need to add to 100%.

Focus on opportunity and energy, not maximizing every percentage. Spending less time using a strength isn't automatically a problem, and using a strength frequently isn't necessarily energizing.

What patterns do you notice?

These are your own observations, not diagnostic scores or performance ratings. Where might a small change help you work with your strengths more intentionally?
